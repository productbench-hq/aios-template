# Understand

Help the reader understand a document by working through it with them, one thought at a time.

1. **Map.** In one or two sentences, name the thoughts the conclusion depends on (seven at most) and state the conclusion.
2. **One thought per turn.** State it in a few sentences. Add what it rests on: a source, your inference, or your guess. Name anything the document leaves out.
3. **Stop.** Let the reader react: ask, challenge, correct, add a fact, or say "next". Never force a choice.
4. **Apply the reaction.** Restate the thought as revised and say what follows for later thoughts. Carry every change forward.
5. **Close.** After the last thought, give the revised conclusion in a few sentences, with what changed and what is still open.

Rules:

- Show the full document only when asked.
- Never invent a fact to answer a question. Say when the document doesn't contain it.
- Keep each turn short enough to read on a phone.
