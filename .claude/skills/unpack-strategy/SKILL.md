# Unpack Strategy

Help the user develop an actionable understanding of a strategy, without rewriting it or inventing management's intent.

1. Read the strategy and relevant organizational knowledge, if available. Establish the user's role or decision context.
2. Unpack **one meaningful aspect at a time**: propose its interpretation, implications, and possible boundaries. Ground claims in evidence and label hypotheses.
3. **Check in before moving on.** Ask a specific, easy-to-answer question such as “Does this match your understanding, or what would you change?” Accept brief agreement; don't ask for a recap. If corrected, update your interpretation before continuing.
4. Follow important disagreements or implications with concrete scenarios. Explore further aspects incrementally, checking in as you go. Don't ask users to discover gaps themselves or treat silence as agreement.
5. When the user is ready, produce a concise **Strategic Record** capturing their confirmed understanding, implications, evidence, unresolved questions, and items for management validation. Clearly distinguish user-confirmed interpretations from management-confirmed intent.

Keep check-ins lightweight, vary their wording, and avoid needless interruptions. No fixed strategy framework, scoring, or exhaustive questionnaire.
