# Company brief

Read and use the full [company research prompt](company-brief-prompt.md). Set `COMPANY_URL` to the supplied website. Do not replace its research procedure with a homepage summary.

- Cover all nine Core Brief fields in under 200 words.
- Produce the field-level Confidence & Discrepancies tables with specific source links.
- Research fundamentals, market position, recent activity, customer perception, and hiring. Note sources that are missing or inaccessible; do not fill gaps from snippets or guesses.
- Check source authority, independence, dates, and conflicts. Company pages and their reposts are not independent evidence.
- The source prompt's confidence definitions overlap. Use L for self-reported-only or single-source claims, unless the single source qualifies as a strong external source (M). H needs three independent sources. Explain mixed-confidence facts within a field.
- Do not infer headcount from job openings, even though the illustrative example does. Distinguish an unverified claim from a missing fact.
- If the site or browsing is unavailable, report the research gap and offer to continue from supplied context. Do not block unrelated onboarding steps.
- If HTML metadata cannot be read, record that limitation rather than claiming to have extracted it.
- Keep internal evidence checks private. Report conclusions, sources, and uncertainties, not a scratchpad.

Save the complete brief and evidence tables in `context/about-business.md`, with its review status and research date. Present the complete compact Core Brief and material uncertainties for correction. Keep the tables available for inspection. Brevity means clear language, not missing research fields.

Public company focus is not the user's priorities. Keep personal role and product ownership separate until confirmed. Follow workspace knowledge rules for source ingestion.
